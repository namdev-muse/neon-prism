How to Play

Tap or click a mirror to rotate it. Mirrors bend the laser beam 90 degrees.

Prisms split the beam in two — straight on, and turned. Walls stop the beam.

Light every receptor at the same time to clear the chamber. Each rotation costs a move — run out of moves and the chamber fails.

Keyboard: arrows or Tab select a mirror, Space rotates, R resets, P pauses, M mutes.
